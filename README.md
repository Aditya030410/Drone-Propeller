DRONE PROPELLER
So, It is a Drone Propeller for custom FPV drones like quadcopter etc. It can be attached to a motor of a drone through the central hub it has in the center.

WHY I MADE IT ?

Actually, I am really into robotics and drones stuff and i always wanted to make one myself and being a part of hackclub community i thought of why to just but i started making things related to it and thinking of making a whole drone as a project in future to get actual grant to make on myself, and as starter, i made this drone propeller for my very own drone i am gonna make in future!!

HOW I MADE IT ?

i am kind of a beginner to CAD, ( although it doesn't look like I am a beginner by seeing that model but still ) and i was looking up for tutorials for a drone propeller on the youtube and thanks to that video by sid lakkoju, It was a really nice tutorial on how to design a propeller in fusion 360. I follwed every single step and figured out stuff on my own too and at the end with the help of that video and smashing my head on random buttons and features i ended up with this beautiful model of a Drone Propeller.
